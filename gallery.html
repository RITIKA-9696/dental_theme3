Case Study

A full smile reconsidered in five gentle visits.

This patient arrived with worn edges, uneven colour, and two older crowns that no longer matched. We took a step back and planned the entire smile — proportion, shade, and shape — before touching a single tooth.

  • Full diagnostic workup with photography and bite analysis
  • Six hand-layered ceramic veneers, upper arch
  • Two replacement crowns, shade-matched to the new veneers
  • Enamel-safe whitening on the lower arch
  • Final polish and review at 6 weeks
