Before Your Visit

Common questions, gently answered.

A few things patients often ask us before their first appointment — feel free to call if you have others.

Call the studio
Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

We recommend booking in advance so you get your preferred time slot. We do accept walk-ins for emergencies, but scheduled patients are seen first.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring a valid ID, any previous dental X-rays or reports, a list of current medications, and insurance details if applicable. Arrive 10 minutes early to complete our patient form.

Is parking available at the studio?

Yes — free parking is available in the building basement for all patients. There is also ample street parking on Lansdowne Terrace.

Do you treat children?

Absolutely. We have a dedicated pediatric dentist and a child-friendly treatment room designed to make young patients feel comfortable and safe.

How soon can I get an appointment?

Typically within 1–2 working days for routine visits. For emergencies, we offer same-day slots — please call ahead so we can reserve a chair for you.

Do you accept insurance?

We work with most major dental insurance providers. Please share your policy details in advance so we can verify coverage before your appointment.
