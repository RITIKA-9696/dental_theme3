How It Started

A small studio built on patience, not speed.

"We never wanted to be the busiest clinic in town — only the calmest one on the street."

Dr. Anika Rao founded Mint & Moss in 2012 after years of working in larger practices where she watched patients feel rushed, unheard, and often afraid. She wanted to build something different — a studio where appointments could stretch, where questions were welcome, and where the whole atmosphere felt less clinical and more like a home.

Today, the studio is still intentionally small. We see fewer patients each day than most practices our size, and we spend longer with each of them. We use modern, gentle techniques — BPA-free composites, low-radiation digital imaging, anxiety-aware anaesthesia — and we plan every treatment as if it were for someone in our own family.

Our patients are adults, teens, and children — often several generations of the same family. Many of them have told us that Mint & Moss is the first place they've actually enjoyed going to the dentist. That, more than anything, is what we set out to build.

Dr. Anika Rao
Founder & Principal Dentist
