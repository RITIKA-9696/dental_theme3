Common Questions

Things patients often ask.

A few answers before your first visit — and if your question isn't here, we're always happy to talk it through.

Ask us anything
Which treatment should I start with?

Almost always, a consultation. We'll examine, listen, and recommend a plan in order of priority — sometimes starting with something simple and preventive before cosmetic work.

Do you treat nervous patients?

Very much so. A large part of our practice is patients who haven't been to a dentist in years. We work at your pace, with longer appointments and gentle techniques.

Are dental implants painful?

The procedure itself is completely painless under local anaesthesia. Post-operative discomfort is typically mild and well-managed with simple pain relief for a few days.

How long does teeth whitening last?

Professional whitening typically lasts one to two years, depending on your diet and habits. Avoiding tobacco, coffee, and red wine — and using a take-home touch-up kit — helps extend the results.

At what age should my child first see a dentist?

We recommend a first visit by age one, or within six months of the first tooth appearing. Early visits help prevent problems and get children comfortable with the dental chair.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — for larger treatment plans we offer staged payments and interest-free instalments. Ask during your consultation and we'll outline the options.
